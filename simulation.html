How to play

Drive BOB through the arena yourself. The wheels, rocker arms, bogies and differential bar move for real as the ground changes under them.

Driving BOB
W S or ↑ ↓forward / reverse
A D or ← →turn on the spot (tank steering)
Shifthold for slow, precise driving
Mcargo flap (drops the medicine packet)
Flying Pip
Plaunch Pip (Bob stopped and level), again to auto-land
Tabswitch control between Bob and Pip
WSADPip forward, back, strafe
Q Eturn Pip
R FPip up / down
Spacedrop one of Pip's 3 packets
Cameras
Cnext camera
1–9Chase, Cinematic, Driver, Wheel, Side, Top, Pip follow, Pip cam, Free

Backspacereset everything
The arena
ConesBob can push them or knock them over. Each knocked cone costs 20 points.
ArenasClassic, Cone slalom, Rubble yard, or a random one from a seed (Mission menu).
Hardwareshows the real parts inside BOB and lets you set the simulator to match them.
Phonetwo sticks (left drives, right turns/lifts Pip), Tilt steers like a wheel. A gamepad works too.

Try this: 1. drive over the logs and blocks and watch the arms. 2. Stop near the casualty, press P, fly Pip over them and drop a packet. 3. Land Pip with P. 4. Drive up the ramp to the glowing drop zone and press M.

Physics

Off = simple arcade driving. On = motor torque, traction, slopes, load and tipping are simulated.

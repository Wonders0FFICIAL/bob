How to play

Drive BOB through the arena yourself. The wheels, rocker arms, bogies and differential bar move for real as the ground changes under them.

Driving BOB
W S or ↑ ↓forward / reverse
A D or ← →turn on the spot (tank steering)
Shifthold for slow, precise driving
Mopen or close the cargo flap (drops the medicine packet)
Flying Pip
Plaunch Pip (Bob must be stopped and level), press again to auto-land
Tabswitch control between Bob and Pip
W S / A DPip forward, back, strafe
Q E or ← →turn Pip
R FPip up / down
Spacedrop one of Pip's 3 packets
Mission, replay, link
Missiontimed run with a score: find the casualty on the thermal camera, drop a packet next to them, deliver the medicine packet, land Pip. Pick an arena seed or press Randomize.
Camsshows Bob's webcam and the 32x24 thermal camera (the casualty glows hot)
Replaywatch your last run again, save it to a file, load it later, or export a video
Linkdrive BOB from your own code over WebSocket (see BOB_sim_API.md)
Phonetwo on-screen sticks (left drives, right turns/lifts Pip), Tilt steers like a wheel. Landscape works best. A gamepad also works: sticks, A launch/land, B drop, X flap, Y switch, bumpers camera.
Other
Cchange camera (Chase, Side, Top, Pip cam, Free)
XX-ray the shell
Backspacereset everything

Try this: 1. drive over the two logs and the blocks and watch the arms. 2. Stop near the casualty (the orange dummy), press P, fly Pip over it and drop a packet. 3. Land Pip with P. 4. Drive up the ramp to the glowing drop zone and press M. On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen buttons.

Physics

Off = simple arcade driving. On = motor torque, traction, slopes, load and tipping are simulated.

    How to use Bob

    You need two devices: a phone that rides on Bob, and a laptop or PC for the control room. Both need internet. Each person or team gets their own passcodes, so your camera and your feed stay private to you. Ask your team lead for yours.

    Device 1 · The phone on Bob

    Start the camera

    1. Open Chrome on the Android phone.
    2. Go to the camera page. Scan the code below, or type bob.jrofficial.org/#camera.
    3. Enter the camera passcode and press Start camera.
    4. Tap Allow when Chrome asks to use the camera.
    5. Wait for the message "Streaming to the control room." Then mount the phone on Bob. The page keeps the screen awake, so leave the phone alone.

    Scan with the phone's camera to open the camera page.

    Using this phone now? Open the camera panel

    Device 2 · The control-room laptop

    Watch and control

    1. Open the site bob.jrofficial.org in any browser.
    2. Go to the Control room with the button at the top of the page.
    3. Enter the viewer passcode and press Connect.
    4. Wait for the green Live badge. If it says "Waiting for Bob's camera", start the phone first (Device 1).
    5. Use the buttons on the right: flip the camera, zoom, save a snapshot, or go full screen.

    Good to know

    • Use Chrome on Android for the phone. Other browsers and iPhones may not support everything.
    • Start with a fully charged phone. A power bank on the robot helps if you can fit one. Turn off battery saver and lower the screen brightness to save power.
    • The page keeps the screen awake by itself, but don't lock the phone or switch to another app. Chrome pauses the camera when the page is hidden.
    • Tick Remember on this device on devices you own, so they reconnect by themselves next time. Don't tick it on shared computers.
    • Several people can watch at the same time. Just open the site on each device and connect.
    • Close the tabs when you're finished. Streaming time is limited each month.
    Something isn't working?
    "Wrong passcode"
    The phone needs the camera passcode. The laptop needs the viewer passcode. They are different.
    "Waiting for Bob's camera"
    The laptop is connected, but the phone hasn't started streaming. Do Device 1.
    Chrome never asks for the camera, or says it's blocked
    Tap the lock icon in the address bar, choose Permissions, set Camera to Allow, then reload the page.
    The picture is black or frozen
    On the phone, close the tab, reopen the camera page, and press Start camera again.
    "Server is not configured yet"
    A website setting is missing. Tell the team member who manages the site.
    No internet at the scene? Use Local network mode
    1. On the phone, open the IP Webcam app and press Start server. Note the address it shows.
    2. Connect the laptop to the same WiFi (for example the phone's hotspot).
    3. Open this site from the team's local copy, not the https address, because browsers block http cameras on https pages.
    4. In the Control room, choose Local network, paste the phone's address, and press Connect.
